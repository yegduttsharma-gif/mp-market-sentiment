PDMP · Crowd Psychology Watchlist

Track what the public is being told (media narrative) separately from what investors are expressing (retail opinion). Extreme consensus is a research alert, not proof of a reversal. No price data used.

How to interpret

80%+ bullish: investigate bearish contrarian possibilities. 20% or less bullish: investigate bullish contrarian possibilities. Both require enough independent directional evidence. A media extreme alone is not a retail extreme.

Media and investor samples are not representative surveys. Coverage on YouTube, X, Facebook or Instagram is only counted when actual public items are collected and attributable; unavailable platforms are never assumed monitored.

Data sources: saved public-news headline collection and public investor-forum discussions. Neutral items are excluded from bullish-share denominators. Index and stock opinion require 10+ directional investor titles. Data collection is scheduled every five minutes but can be delayed or blocked by sources. No unverified historical changes or invented confidence percentages.
