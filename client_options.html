What this measures

Call Long = outstanding bought/held index call contracts in NSE's Client category. Put Long = outstanding bought/held index put contracts. It is NOT the number of people buying options today, not live buy orders, and not a pure retail-only statistic.

The report combines all index options (not Nifty-only or Bank Nifty-only). Some positions may be hedges or parts of spreads, so the larger side does not necessarily mean that most retail traders expect that direction.

Data updates after NSE publishes a daily report and the dashboard collector successfully retrieves it. Missing data is not estimated.
