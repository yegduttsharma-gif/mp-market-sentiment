Volatility-based estimate for Nifty 50 and Sensex. This calculator automatically loads available public quote snapshots, with optional manual overrides. Quotes may be delayed or stale; verify current exchange prices before trading.

Reference: NSE India VIX methodology. This is a theoretical one-trading-day, one-standard-deviation close-to-close move, not an intraday high-low forecast.

How to interpret

Daily move (%) ≈ India VIX ÷ √252. Expected move (points) ≈ index level × daily move (%). Lower/upper levels are index level ± expected move. Under an idealized normal-return assumption, a ±1σ band contains about 68% of one-day closing returns; actual market returns can be fat-tailed and the probability is not guaranteed. The VIX is derived from Nifty options and its application to Sensex is an approximation. VIX does not forecast direction.
